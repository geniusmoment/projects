# Modular glass lighting concept

Date: 2026-11-18. Coursework concept, version 1.0.

## Design idea

A repairable low-voltage lighting platform for a cream and burgundy living room. A common mechanical rail holds detachable clear-glass modules and replaceable 24 V LED light engines. The same rail can use individual glass drops or curved fluted-glass screens. Glass, metal, drivers and light engines can be detached without destroying adjacent parts. Ambient, reading and decorative light groups are switched separately. The existing sofa, chairs, walnut table and wall finishes are retained.

This is a conceptual specification, not a tested electrical product or a completed photometric calculation. This timestamp records the concept and the identified image versions; it does not establish exclusive copyright over AI-generated content. The room visualizations were made with ChatGPT image generation following a human brief and iterative direction.

## Five visual explorations

1. Alabaster petals and diffused ambient light.
2. Transparent glass spheres and layered reflections.
3. Linear architectural fixtures and wall grazing.
4. Clear-glass droplet cascade.
5. Curved fluted-glass ribbons.

The five images explore different lighting families. The modular rail described above is a new design proposition developed from the glass-focused explorations, not a claim that every pictured fixture already uses this engineering.
